A Space Reimagined with Heart and Art

Long before Cafe Pink, this spot in Hauz Khas Village was known for its iconic graffiti wall. It has since appeared in films and series, and has been a favourite location for brand shoots, influencer reels and music videos.

Today it is owned by Poonam Sabarwal, who nurtures the café like her own child, and has been beautifully reimagined by Oval Art Studio. The new design blends art, comfort and creativity for a warm, cozy and visually vibrant vibe, making it one of Hauz Khas Village's most inviting destinations.
